Common mistakes

Why your ads
are burning
budget.

The same handful of setup mistakes drain campaigns regardless of industry. Here are the most common ones, and how we fix them.

Problem

You're targeting too broadly and showing ads to everyone, including people who'll never buy.

Fix

Use exact and phrase match keywords only. Precision beats volume every time.

Problem

You have no negative keyword list, so you're paying for searches that were never relevant.

Fix

Exclude irrelevant searches before you spend a cent. It's free to filter, and it saves a fortune.

Problem

You're optimising for clicks, not conversions. Traffic means nothing if it doesn't convert.

Fix

Switch to a conversion-based bidding strategy. Let the data drive the spend, not assumptions.
